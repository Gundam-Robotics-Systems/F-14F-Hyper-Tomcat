System Integration & Mobile App Delivery

To make the Sea Spear fully functional without modifying the F-14's legacy instrumentation, the system links the weapon's tracking sensors straight to the **Cockpit Tablet App** over our wireless loop:

1.  **System Handshake (The Aegis Bridge):** When loaded onto the BRU-32 rack, the triple pylon pins engage the backplane matrix. The **Univac-Aegis Bridge (Slot 3)** intercepts the missile's modern ethernet digital networks, wrapping the tracking vectors into a format readable by the jet's core processing loop. [[1](http://www.anft.net/f-14/f14-detail-rack-bomb.htm)]
2.  **Multi-Target Cockpit Selection:** The app screen automatically shifts to show an active weapon module slot. The RIO uses the touch panel to view the missile's live millimeter-wave radar profile, allowing the crew to manually select or automatically paint swarming surface craft simultaneously. [[1](https://mbdainc.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/2018-BRIMSTONE-datasheet.pdf), 2, [3](https://missilethreat.csis.org/missile/brimstone/)]
3.  **Fire-and-Forget Release Execution:** When the pilot hits the cockpit stick release button, a fast ignition pulse fires the launcher rail. The missile drops away safely, instantly turning on its internal solid-fuel rocket engine to home in on targets autonomously.
