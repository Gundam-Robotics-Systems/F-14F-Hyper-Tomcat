📡 1. Synthetic Micro-AESA Sonar Processing Pod

-   **The Legacy Failure:** The original F-14 completely lacked organic anti-submarine or sub-surface tracking, relying on off-board fleet data relays.
-   **The Upgrade:** A self-contained, belly-mounted **Synthetic Micro-AESA Sonar Processing Pod** that interfaces directly with dropped sonobuoy telemetry over our wireless backplane.

💡 2. All-LED Solid-State Exterior & Interior Lighting Array

-   **The Legacy Failure:** Incandescent position, landing, and formation lights (like the fragile wing-tip slimes) burned out constantly due to high-G flight vibrations and deck-slam landing impact shocks.
-   **The Upgrade:** High-intensity **Gallium-Nitride (GaN) solid-state LEDs** housed in ruggedized, vibration-isolated polyether ether ketone (PEEK) lenses.

📻 3. Frequency-Hopping Software-Defined Radio (SDR) Transceiver

-   **The Legacy Failure:** The original analog radios relied on mechanical tuning crystals and vacuum tubes that drifted out of calibration, making them highly vulnerable to modern electronic jamming.
-   **The Upgrade:** A ruggedized, wide-band **SDR tactical transceiver** that runs multi-channel encrypted waveforms and frequency-hopping protocols straight out of our drop-in chassis.

⛽ 4. Brushless Digital Fuel Boost Pump

-   **The Legacy Failure:** Legacy fuel pumps used mechanical brushes that eroded rapidly from friction, leading to engine fuel-starvation faults or vapor locks during rapid, high-altitude climbs.
-   **The Upgrade:** A completely sealed, **sensorless brushless digital pump** that regulates fuel velocity electronically, utilizing self-lubricating ceramic bearings to tolerate extreme temperature drops.

🧈 5. Solenoid-Driven Positive Displacement Grease Pump

-   **The Legacy Failure:** The critical wing-sweep pivot points required frequent manual, messy lubrication overhauls by ground crews to prevent metal-on-metal seizing.
-   **The Upgrade:** An automated, **solenoid-driven positive displacement grease pump** that injects micro-metered pulses of low-temperature synthetic lubricants into the wing-pivot bearings during dynamic sweep transitions.

🛢️ 6. CNC-Milled Billet Aluminum Oil Pan with Neodymium Magnetic Trap

-   **The Legacy Failure:** Cast aluminum oil pans cracked under intense carrier landing stress loads, and original filtration screens let microscopic metal shavings cycle back into the engine, causing premature bearing failure.
-   **The Upgrade:** A deep-profile, **CNC-milled 7075-T6 billet aluminum oil pan** equipped with a massive, high-temperature **Neodymium (NdFeB) magnetic core drain trap** to capture internal engine wear debris before it cycles.
