Part 1: OpenSCAD Triple-Rail Sea Spear Launch Pylon (`sea_spear_triple_rack.scad`)

This script models a high-speed, lightweight composite launcher pylon. It features top-facing **14-inch military suspension hooks** to seat cleanly into the F-14's BRU-32 rack, while managing three separate bottom-facing missile launching tracks spaced evenly to clear exhaust gases.
