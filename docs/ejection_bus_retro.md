OpenSCAD Digital Ejection Sequence Controller Adapter (`ejection_bus_retro.scad`)

To bring the ejection infrastructure into our modernization loop without modifying the primary mechanical rocket seats, we can build a non-destructive **Ejection Sequence Umbilical Tap Panel**.

This script models a small, drop-in breakout enclosure that mounts behind the seat rails. It lets our **Univac IX core module** monitor seat armed states and deploy parameters over the mobile application without touching the explosive ballistic path.
